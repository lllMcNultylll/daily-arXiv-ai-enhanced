### [1] [Bridging Solar and Stellar Physics: Role of SDO in Understanding Stellar Active Regions and Atmospheric Heating](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16779)
*Shin Toriumi*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 该综述论文探讨了利用太阳动力学天文台（SDO）数据将太阳作为恒星进行研究，以理解恒星磁活动和大气加热机制，为系外行星环境提供预测工具。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 建立太阳-恒星连接框架，利用太阳的详细观测数据来理解更广泛恒星谱系中的磁活动和大气加热过程，弥合太阳物理与恒星物理之间的鸿沟。

Method: 利用NASA太阳动力学天文台（SDO）的三个仪器（HMI、AIA、EVE）获取高分辨率太阳观测数据，将空间分辨的太阳观测整合为盘积分数据集，模拟恒星观测条件。

Result: 成功表征了活动区特征，量化了普适的加热关系，重建了恒星X射线和紫外光谱，为理解恒星磁活动提供了新见解，并为系外行星环境预测提供了工具。

Conclusion: 太阳作为恒星的研究框架有效推进了对恒星磁活动的理解，为系外行星环境预测提供了实用工具，并指出了将太阳为基础的研究框架应用于不同恒星群体的未来方向。

Abstract: The solar-stellar connection provides a unique framework for understanding magnetic activity and atmospheric heating across a broad spectrum of stars. Solar Dynamics Observatory (SDO) of NASA, equipped with the Helioseismic and Magnetic Imager, Atmospheric Imaging Assembly, and Extreme ultraviolet Variability Experiment, has enabled detailed Sun-as-a-star studies that bridge solar and stellar physics. Integrating spatially resolved solar observations into disk-integrated datasets, these studies provide insights into magnetic activity occurring in distant stars. This review highlights key results from recent analyses that employed all three SDO instruments to characterize active regions, quantify universal heating relationships, and reconstruct stellar X-ray and ultraviolet spectra. We discuss how these findings advance our understanding of stellar magnetic activity, provide predictive tools for exoplanetary environments, and outline future directions for applying solar-based frameworks to diverse stellar populations.

### [2] [Gaia24ccy: An outburst followed the footsteps of its predecessor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16791)
*Koshvendra Singh,Joe P. Ninan,Zhen Guo,Valentin D. Ivanov,David A. H. Buckley,Devendra K. Ojha,Andrew Monson,Tarak Chand,Saurabh Sharma,Ram Kesh Yadav,Devendra K. Sahu,Pramod Kumar,Vardan Elbakyan,Sergei Nayakshin,Vitor Fermiano,Min Fang,Jura Borissova,Wen Ping Chen,Franz-Josef Hambsch,Radostin Kurtev,Calum Morris,Javier Osses,Vania Rodriguez,Tanvi Sharma,Bandari Srikanth,Thanawuth Thanathibodee,Wei-Hao Wang,Yuting Zhou*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 研究年轻恒星系统Gaia24ccy的双星爆发事件，发现其具有独特的两阶段爆发模式，持续时间长且包含多个子爆发，提出了热粘滞触发与磁层压缩的混合模型解释


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 年轻恒星吸积驱动的爆发事件样本有限，限制了对其物理机制的理解，因此需要对每个爆发系统进行深入研究。Gaia24ccy系统表现出两次独特的爆发事件，为研究这类现象提供了宝贵机会。

Method: 通过光变曲线分析、周期图分析确定爆发源；利用爆发上升/衰减时间尺度和中红外颜色演化推断触发机制；通过发射线光谱分析磁层活动；计算吸积质量和触发区域位置。

Result: 确定Gaia24ccy B为爆发源；发现两次爆发具有相似的两阶段演化模式：前15天快速上升，随后出现10-20天时间尺度的多个子爆发；爆发持续时间分别为145-255天和367天；触发区域位于0.019-0.047 au（约5-12.3倍恒星半径）；每次事件吸积质量约10^{-5} M⊙；中红外颜色红化由粘滞盘和光球竞争发射导致。

Conclusion: Gaia24ccy的爆发特征支持热粘滞触发与磁层压缩的混合模型，不同于已知的EXor源，为年轻恒星吸积爆发机制提供了新见解，强调了内盘热粘滞不稳定性与磁层活动共同作用的重要性。

Abstract: Accretion-driven outbursts in young stellar objects remain poorly understood, largely limited by a statistically small sample of closely followed-up events. This underscores the importance of a thorough exploration of each outbursting object. We studied a peculiar outbursting system, Gaia24ccy, which exhibited two $Δg \sim$ 3.8 mag outbursts in 2019 and 2024. The system consists of two unresolved, nearly identical, and rapidly rotating young stars: Gaia24ccy A (1.1419 days) and Gaia24ccy B (1.7898 days). Periodogram analyses just before the onset of the outbursts suggest Gaia24ccy B to be the outbursting component. Unlike any previously known EXor sources, the two outburst profiles show a very similar evolution: both rose at the same rate for the first 15 days, followed by many 'sub-bursts' on the timescale of 10-20 days. The 2019 outburst lasted 145-255 days, while the 2024 outburst persisted for 367 days. We infer the unstable region to lie at $r_{\rm trigger} \simeq 0.019-0.047$ au ($\sim5-12.3 R_\star$). The accreted mass per event $M_{\rm acc}\sim10^{-5} M_\odot$ can be provided by a compact inner-disk reservoir. The photometric rise/decay timescales and the mid-infrared color evolution favor a thermal-viscous trigger in a hot inner disk, while the appearance of rich emission-line spectra indicates concurrent magnetospheric compression - together best described by a hybrid picture. Finally, we explain the reddening of the mid-infrared color observed during the outburst as a consequence of the competing emission from the viscous disk and the photosphere.

### [3] [Stellar Paternity Tests: Matching High-Latitude B Stars to the Open Clusters of their Birth](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16925)

TL;DR: 利用Gaia DR3数据追踪39颗高银纬B型星和447个银河系疏散星团的运动轨迹，寻找过去交汇时刻，通过运动学分析和HR图年龄匹配确认了5颗"孤儿"B型星的起源，并讨论了可能的抛射机制。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: OB星通常在银河系薄盘内的疏散星团中形成，当它们出现在高银纬时，被认为是在过去几千万年内从其诞生星团中被抛射出来的。研究这些高银纬B型星的起源有助于理解恒星抛射机制和银河系动力学。

Method: 使用Gaia DR3的高精度天体测量数据，追踪39颗高银纬B型星和447个银河系疏散星团的运动轨迹，寻找过去可能的交汇时刻。对轨迹匹配的案例，进一步分析星团的HR图以确认年龄匹配，并分析星团核心环境来确定可能的抛射机制。

Result: 通过运动学分析和年龄匹配，成功识别了5颗"孤儿"B型星的可能起源。确定了每颗星的旅行时间、抛射速度，并讨论了各自的逃逸机制。还发现一颗星在分析时间范围内轨迹未接近银盘，讨论了其可能的其他起源。

Conclusion: 通过综合运动学轨迹分析、年龄匹配和星团环境评估，能够有效识别高银纬B型星的起源。这些"孤儿"星很可能是从疏散星团中被抛射出来的，研究为理解恒星抛射机制和银河系动力学提供了重要线索。

Abstract: OB stars generally form in open clusters within the Milky Way's thin disk, so when they are found at high Galactic latitudes, it is thought that they were ejected from their birth clusters during the past few tens of millions of years. Using Gaia Data Release 3 (hereafter DR3) data, we traced the kinematic trajectories of 39 high-latitude B-type stars and 447 Galactic open clusters with high-quality astrometry to search for moments of past intersection. In cases where we found matching trajectories, we also considered the clusters' HR diagrams to confirm parent-orphan pairs have matching ages. Further analysis of the clusters' core environments allowed us to determine a probable ejection mechanism. Through these paternity tests, we have identified possible origins for five of these orphaned B-type stars. Here we present the likely travel times, ejection velocities, and a discussion of the runaway mechanism for each case. We also identify one star whose trajectory did not bring it near the disk during the time period of our analysis, and we discuss its possible origins as well.

### [4] [Multiple shocks generated by the 2024 May 14 coronal mass ejection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16983)
*Mohamed Nedal,Catherine Cuddy,David M. Long,Shilpi Bhunia,Pietro Zucca,Peter T. Gallagher*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 该研究通过多仪器观测分析2014年5月14日CME事件相关的四个II型射电暴，发现它们产生于CME侧翼的不同日冕高度，由超阿尔芬激波在开放磁场线和低阿尔芬速度区域形成。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究CME相关的II型射电暴的生成机制和位置，特别是多个II型暴如何从单个CME事件中产生，以及日冕条件对激波形成的影响。

Method: 结合GOES-SUVI的白光和极紫外观测、SOHO-LASCO的两个日冕仪观测，以及I-LOFAR的10-240MHz地面射电观测。使用PFSS和MHD模型分析日冕等离子体条件，通过密度模型确定射电源的径向距离。

Result: 识别出四个II型射电暴，具有频带分裂、鱼骨结构和碎片化特征。激波速度范围443-2075 km/s，漂移率-361至-78 kHz/s，阿尔芬马赫数约3.21-3.57（超阿尔芬）。第一个II型暴在CME发射后约18分钟触发，每个暴产生于日冕不同高度。

Conclusion: II型射电暴很可能产生于CME侧翼附近，该区域开放磁场线和相对较低的阿尔芬速度有利于激波形成。多仪器研究表明单个CME可以产生多个不同日冕高度的II型暴。

Abstract: This study characterises a series of type~II radio bursts associated with a CME that occurred on 14 May, focusing on the coronal conditions during the event and identifying the likely location of the shocks where the radio bursts are generated. The CME was tracked using a combination of white light and extreme ultraviolet observations of the solar corona taken by three instruments: GOES-SUVI, two coronagraphs of the SOHO-LASCO, together with ground-based radio observations between 10-240~MHz from I-LOFAR. The radial distances of the radio sources were examined using a series of density models, with both PFSS and MHD models used to examine the coronal plasma conditions. Four type~II bursts were identified in the I$-$LOFAR radio dynamic spectrum over $\sim$15~minutes, exhibiting features such as band splitting, herringbones, and fragmentation. The shocks were found to have speeds ranging between $\sim$443$-$2075~km s$^{-1}$, with drift rates of $\sim-$361 to -78~kHz~s$^{-1}$. The shocks were found to have a $M_A \approx$ 3.21$-$3.57. indicating that they were super-Alfvénic. The first type~II burst was triggered $\sim$18~minutes after the CME launch, with each burst appearing to have been generated at a different height in the corona. Analysis of the derived kinematics and modelling results suggests that the type~II bursts were likely produced at the shoulders of the CME near the flanks, where open magnetic field lines and relatively low Alfvén speeds facilitated shock formation. This multi-instrument study shows that multiple type II bursts from a single CME originated at different coronal heights, with modelling indicating their generation near the CME flanks, where low Alfvén speeds and open magnetic field lines facilitated shock formation.

### [5] [Lithium as a probe of stellar and galactic physics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17470)
*C. Charbonnel,N. Prantzos*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 综述性论文，系统回顾了锂在恒星演化、银河系考古学和宇宙学中的观测、理论模型、来源及演化历史


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 锂在天体物理学中具有独特地位，是研究低质量恒星物理与演化、银河系考古学和宇宙学的强大诊断工具。本文旨在全面回顾锂在不同演化阶段恒星中的观测数据、当前理论模型的优势与局限、锂的来源理解以及锂在银河系历史中的演化

Method: 采用文献综述方法，系统分析锂在不同演化阶段恒星中的观测数据，评估当前恒星演化模型在解释锂丰度数据方面的能力，综合研究锂的来源机制及其在银河系历史中的演化过程

Result: 1) 考虑化学元素和角动量基本输运过程的恒星演化模型有望为所有银河系恒星种群（包括锂丰度凹陷和平原）提供统一的锂消耗解释；2) 新星很可能是银河系中锂的主要来源（基于观测证据但理论尚未完全确立）；3) 恒星在银河系盘中的径向迁移是理解银河系锂演化许多方面的关键

Conclusion: 锂作为天体物理学的重要示踪元素，其观测数据与理论模型的结合为理解恒星演化、银河系化学演化和宇宙学提供了关键见解。未来研究需要进一步完善理论模型，特别是关于锂的来源机制和恒星在银河系中的迁移过程

Abstract: Lithium plays a unique role in astrophysics, as it is a powerful diagnostic for the physics and evolution of low-mass stars, Galactic archaeology, and cosmology. We review the Li observations in stars at different phases of their evolution, the strengths and the limitations of the current theoretical stellar models to explain the Li abundance data, our understanding of the Li sources and of the evolution of Li through- out the Galactic history. Key takeaways from the current state of the research in the field are: 1) Stellar evolution models accounting for fundamental transport processes of chemical species and angular momentum hold the promise of providing a common stellar Li depletion explanation to the Li abundance patterns observed in all Galactic stellar populations, including the dip and the plateau(s). 2) Novae are most probably the main source of Li in the Galaxy, on observational (but not yet theoretically established) grounds. 3) Radial migration of stars in the Galactic disk holds the key to understand many aspects of the Li evolution in the Milky Way.
