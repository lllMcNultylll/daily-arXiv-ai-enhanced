To model the wind, we used our global wind code that calculates the radiative force from the solution of the radiative transfer equation in the comoving frame. Wind models calculated for particular stellar parameters reproduce the observational mass-loss rates better than simple scaling relations derived from model grids. This indicates that the dependence of the mass-loss rate on the other stellar parameters except the luminosity might explain part of the dispersion in the relation between the mass-loss rate and luminosity. However, some dispersion still remains, which might indicate that wind variability is another source of the dispersion.
