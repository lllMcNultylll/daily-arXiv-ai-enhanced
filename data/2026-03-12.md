<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 解决太阳宁静日冕中两种独立电子温度诊断方法（射电亮度温度与流体静力学标尺高度模型）之间存在的系统性差异问题，该差异因子R=2.4±0.3在八年数据中持续存在且不随太阳周期变化。

Method: 通过分析Nancay射电日像仪和LOFAR的射电亮度温度数据，结合流体静力学标尺高度模型，排除湍流散射等传播效应，提出非麦克斯韦电子速度分布（kappa分布）作为解释，计算kappa/(kappa-3/2)的理论比值。

Result: 观测到的R=2.4对应kappa~2-3的电子速度分布，这与活动区光谱测量一致，但与微扰理论预测的kappa~10-25存在矛盾；预测活动区核心因碰撞性增强应显示R≤1.5。

Conclusion: 太阳宁静日冕中的电子温度差异反映了非麦克斯韦速度分布（kappa~2-3），这意味着对这类等离子体应用Spitzer-Harm传导等流体输运方程在物理上无效，相关的热通量计算仍是未解决问题。

Abstract: Two independent electron temperature diagnostics applied to the quiet solar corona yield systematically different results. Radio brightness temperatures from the Nancay Radioheliograph indicate T_e ~ 0.6 MK, while hydrostatic scale-height modeling of the same plasma requires T_e ~ 1.5 MK (Mercier & Chambe 2015). Both diagnostics probe electrons; they disagree by a factor of R = 2.4 +/- 0.3. This discrepancy persists across an eight-year dataset spanning solar minimum and is consistent with LOFAR observations at lower frequencies (Vocks et al. 2018). We propose that the residual, cycle-invariant discrepancy reflects non-Maxwellian electron velocity distributions. Radio bremsstrahlung samples the distribution core, while ionization rates and scale heights are dominated by the suprathermal tail. For kappa distributions, the predicted ratio is kappa/(kappa - 3/2); the observed R = 2.4 implies kappa ~ 2-3. This is consistent with spectroscopic measurements in active regions but in tension with perturbative theoretical predictions of kappa ~ 10-25. We make falsifiable predictions: Active Region cores should show a collapsed ratio (R <= 1.5) as collisionality restores thermal equilibrium. Applying fluid transport equations (Spitzer-Harm conductivity) to plasmas with kappa ~ 2-3 is physically invalid, but we do not compute the resulting heat flux, which remains an open problem.
