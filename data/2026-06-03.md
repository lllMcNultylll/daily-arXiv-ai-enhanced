### [1] [J0404+1112: A 3-Hour Eclipsing White Dwarf-Brown Dwarf Probing Multiple Atmospheric Regimes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03737)
*J. de Wit,R. Alonso,B. V. Rackham,S. M. Lederer,A. Y. Burdanov,S. L. Casewell,Y. Zhou,J. R. French,K. Barkaoui,M. Albornoz,A. J. Burgasser,T. Cavalier,B. -O. Demory,E. Ducrot,M. Gillon,Y. Gómez Maqueo Chew,M. J. Hooton,P. P. Pedersen,D. Queloz,M. Timmermans,A. H. M. J. Triaud,S. Zúñiga-Fernández*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 本文对J0404+1112这一紧密、全食的白矮星-褐矮星双星系统进行了时间分辨的食变测光和视向速度确认，修正了系统参数：热DA白矮星（Teff≈28,000 K，M≈0.6 M⊙，R≈0.015 R⊙）与质量~40 M_Jup、半径~0.9 R_Jup的褐矮星互绕，轨道周期2.93小时。全食现象使得褐矮星光通量得以分离，该系统为JWST研究强辐照大气热再分配和光化学提供了独特基准。

Motivation: WD+BD双星是研究亚恒星天体在恒星主序后演化中能否存活以及强辐照大气物理的稀有实验室。J0404+1112最初仅以研究简报形式报道，缺乏精确参数，存在白矮星温度、半径、距离和消光之间的强协方差问题，需要打破这一简并。

Method: 使用高时间分辨率的食变测光观测解析白矮星全食，结合光谱、宽带测光、Gaia视差数据进行综合分析。通过归一化Balmer线轮廓拟合和高节奏食变测光（几何约束白矮星半径）打破参数简并。

Result: 确定了热DA白矮星的精确参数（Teff≈28,000 K，log g≈8.0，M≈0.6 M⊙，R≈0.015 R⊙）和褐矮星参数（~40 M_Jup，半径~0.9 R_Jup）。全食观测分离出褐矮星通量，约束了其夜侧辐射。测得昼侧和夜侧亮度温度分别约3,600 K和1,800 K。

Conclusion: J0404+1112是一个紧密全食WD+BD双星系统，参数精确度显著优于此前基于SED的估计。其极短的轨道周期（~3小时）和强辐照条件使其成为JWST研究高温超热木星大气热再分配和光化学的独特高灵敏度基准。

Abstract: White dwarf-brown dwarf (WD+BD) binaries are rare laboratories for probing both substellar survival through post-main-sequence evolution and the physics of strongly irradiated atmospheres. We present time-resolved eclipse photometry and radial-velocity confirmation of J0404+1112, a compact ($P = 2.93$ hr), totally eclipsing WD+BD system initially reported in a Research Note of the AAS. New high-cadence observations resolve a total eclipse of the WD and, combined with spectroscopy, broadband photometry, and the Gaia parallax, yield a revised system architecture. We find a hot DA WD with $T_{\rm eff} \simeq 28{,}000$ K, $\log g \simeq 8.0$, $M_{\rm WD} \simeq 0.6 \rm{M}_\odot$, and $R_{\rm WD} \simeq 0.015 \rm{R}_\odot$, orbited by a $\sim40 \rm{M}_{\rm Jup}$ BD with radius $\sim0.9 \rm{R}_{\rm Jup}$. These parameters supersede earlier SED-based estimates in which the WD temperature, radius, distance, and extinction were strongly covariant. We break this degeneracy with normalized Balmer-line profile fitting and high-cadence eclipse photometry that constraints the WD's radius geometrically. The total eclipses enable isolation of the BD flux and constrain its nightside emission. With its short orbital period and strong irradiation, J0404+1112 provides a uniquely compact, high-sensitivity JWST benchmark for probing atmospheric heat redistribution and photochemistry in the temperature regime of the hottest ultra-hot Jupiters, spanning dayside and nightside brightness temperatures of $\sim3{,}600$ K and $\sim1{,}800$ K within a single $\sim$3 hr orbit.
