### [1] [Statistical Analysis of Minifilament Eruptions Using Full-disk H$α$ Blue-wing Observations at Big Bear Solar Observatory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20612)
*Artin Khaleghi,Qin Li,Nengyi Huang,Jeongwoo Lee,Haimin Wang*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 本文利用大熊湖太阳观测站高频率全日面Hα蓝翼数据，开发了结合强度阈值和时间追踪的算法，在4小时内识别出1986次微暗条爆发事件，给出了其发生频率、长度、寿命的统计分布，并探讨了其与活动区、冕洞边界的空间关系及对太阳风小尺度结构的潜在贡献。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 微暗条爆发对日冕动力学和太阳风结构形成具有重要作用，但以往研究受限于样本量小或视场受限，缺乏对其全球发生率和特征性质的系统性认识。

Method: 利用大熊湖太阳观测站高频率全日面Hα蓝翼图像的强度阈值化与时间追踪算法，检测Hα蓝翼图像中的突然增强事件；随后使用Hα线心观测进行交叉验证，确认相关暗条结构；统计事件发生率、长度、寿命等特征，并结合长度分布、时长-长度尺度关系、活动区邻近性及冕洞边界分布进行分析。

Result: 在2020年6月9日的4小时内识别出1986个事件，得到约6.6×10^4/天的发生率、平均长度约17 Mm、典型寿命约21分钟。长度分布呈幂律（斜率约-4.8），时长与长度呈亚线性标度关系；临近太阳黑子区的爆发事件平均大于全球均值；冕洞内活动受到轻度抑制，边界区域爆发频率居中。

Conclusion: 微暗条爆发的统计特征表明其在小尺度太阳风结构（如磁开关back和磁通量绳）的形成中可能发挥重要作用，为理解日冕-太阳风耦合提供了定量基础。

Abstract: This paper presents a comprehensive statistical analysis of minifilament eruptions (MFEs) using high-cadence full-disk H$α$ blue-wing observations from Big Bear Solar Observatory. Despite the recognized importance of MFEs for coronal dynamics and solar wind structuring, previous efforts were often limited by small sample sizes or restricted fields of view, leaving open questions about their global occurrence and characteristic properties. We developed an algorithm incorporating intensity thresholding and temporal tracking to detect sudden enhancements in H$α$ blue-wing images. A total of 1986 such events were identified during a 4 hr period on 2020 June 9. These detections were cross-validated using H$α$ line-center observations to confirm the presence of associated filament structures. The analysis yields an occurrence rate of $\sim$6.6$\times 10^4$ per day, an average length of $\sim$17 Mm, and a typical lifetime of $\sim$21 minutes. A power-law distribution in eruption lengths (with slope $\sim$-4.8) and a sublinear scaling between duration and length suggest that larger eruptions tend to last longer. The length distribution of MFEs was further analyzed relative to active region proximity, revealing that eruptions occurring nearby a sunspot region were, on average, larger than the global mean eruption length. Additionally, an eruption density map was used to analyze the spatial distribution of MFEs relative to coronal hole boundaries. The results indicate a mild suppression of activity inside coronal holes and intermediate eruption frequency in the boundary regions. We briefly discuss their potential role in structuring the small-scale solar wind features such as magnetic switchbacks and small-scale magnetic flux ropes.

### [2] [BSN: Light Curve Modeling and Orbital Period Analysis of the Contact Binaries OV Leo and V339 Leo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20772)
*Razieh Aliakbari,Atila Poro,Raul Michel,Sabrina Baudart*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 本文对两个相接双星系统OV Leo和V339 Leo进行了联合测光和轨道周期分析，利用地面观测数据和TESS测光，通过BSN软件结合MCMC方法建模。结果显示两者均为W次型浅相接双星，OV Leo发生全食且无星斑，V339 Leo发生偏食并需用冷星斑解释O'Connell效应。轨道周期分析显示两者具有相反的长期周期变化，暗示质量转移正在进行，并通过Gaia视差推算了绝对参数。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 对相接双星系统进行深入研究有助于理解其结构演化、质量转移和角动量损失等物理过程。OV Leo此前从未进行过测光分析，而V339 Leo需要用更新的观测数据和改进的建模方法重新检验，以获得更精确的系统参数和演化信息。

Method: 利用地面观测与TESS测光获取光变曲线，使用BSN软件结合马尔可夫链蒙特卡洛（MCMC）方法进行光变曲线建模，得到系统参数及其不确定性；通过冷星斑模型再现V339 Leo的O'Connell效应；基于食时刻提取进行轨道周期分析，并结合Gaia视差与标准天体物理方程推导绝对参数。

Result: OV Leo和V339 Leo均为W次型浅相接双星；OV Leo首次进行测光分析，发生全食且无需星斑模型；V339 Leo存在O'Connell效应，需用冷星斑模型解释，且发生偏食。轨道周期分析显示两个系统呈现相反的长期周期变化，表明在守恒质量转移假设下存在方向相反的质量转移。

Conclusion: 两个相接双星系统处于浅相接构型，OV Leo与V339 Leo的周期变化方向相反，支持正在进行的质量转移过程。结合Gaia视差导出的绝对参数为理解这类相接双星的物理特性与演化状态提供了新的观测约束。

Abstract: We present a combined photometric and orbital period analysis of the contact binary systems OV Leo and V339 Leo using ground-based observations together with TESS photometry. The light curves were modeled with the BSN software through a Markov Chain Monte Carlo approach, providing physical estimates of the system parameters and their uncertainties. OV Leo is analyzed photometrically for the first time, while V339 Leo is reexamined using new observations and an updated modeling methodology. The light curve analysis show that both targets are W-subtype contact binaries in shallow-contact configurations. The O'Connell effect detected in V339 Leo is reproduced with a cool starspot model, whereas no spot is required for OV Leo. Three-dimensional geometric models further indicate that OV Leo undergoes total eclipses, while V339 Leo exhibits partial eclipses. Orbital period analysis based on eclipse timing extractions reveals opposite long-term period variations in the two systems, suggesting ongoing mass transfer under the assumption of conservative mass transfer. The absolute parameters were derived using the Gaia parallaxes, the photometric light curve solution, and standard astrophysical equations.

### [3] [The DIRECD coronal mass ejection direction catalog: three-dimensional propagation inferred from coronal dimmings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20908)
*Shantanu Jain,Tatiana Podladchikova,Karin Dissauer,Astrid Veronig,Amaia Razquin*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 本文介绍DIRECD日冕物质抛射（CME）方向目录，该目录利用SDO/AIA 211 Å日冕暗化观测重建三维CME传播方向，包含2010-2026年64个事件。统计分析发现低日冕CME传播存在系统性不对称：经向倾角呈现随源纬度增加的向极偏转趋势，而赤道向倾角与源经度无显著依赖。将DIRECD方向引入CME到达预测后，地磁风暴强度预测相关性从r=0.58提升至r=0.70。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: CME是地球空间天气扰动的主要驱动因素之一，但受日冕仪掩食和投影效应影响，其在低日冕的早期传播方向长期缺乏精确约束，亟需从低日冕直接推断CME传播方向的替代诊断方法。

Method: 基于日冕暗化现象，采用DIRECD方法从SDO/AIA 211 Å观测中重建CME锥体几何结构，推导三维传播方向。目录整合了31个太阳周25期（2021-2026）事件与33个前期研究事件，报告每个事件的三维传播方向、二维倾角、角宽度和锥体高度，并进行统计分析和预报验证。

Result: 统计分析揭示低日冕CME传播存在系统性经向不对称：向极偏转在高源纬度更显著，而赤道向倾角与源经度无关。将DIRECD方向纳入CME到达预报后，与实测地磁风暴强度的相关性从r=0.58提升至r=0.70。

Conclusion: 低日冕CME传播路径主要由局部活动区磁拓扑控制，大尺度日冕磁场和日球电流片在更远日心距离才逐步主导。DIRECD目录及软件公开可用，为空间天气研究和CME到达预报提供重要支持。

Abstract: Coronal mass ejections (CMEs) are among the primary drivers of space weather disturbances at Earth, yet their early propagation in the low corona remains poorly constrained owing to occultation and projection effects inherent to coronagraph observations. Coronal dimmings offer an alternative diagnostic to infer CME propagation direction directly from the low corona. We present the DIRECD CME Direction Catalog, a unified dataset of three-dimensional CME propagation directions derived via the DIRECD (Dimming Inferred Estimation of CME Direction) method, which reconstructs CME cone geometry from SDO/AIA 211 Å coronal dimming observations. The catalog comprises 64 events spanning 2010-2026, combining 31 Solar Cycle 25 (2021-2026) events with 33 events from prior studies. For each event we report the 3D propagation direction, 2D inclination angles, angular width, and cone height. Statistical analysis reveals a systematic asymmetry in low-coronal CME propagation: meridional inclinations exhibit a systematic tendency toward latitude-dependent deflection, with poleward deflections more pronounced at higher source latitudes, while equatorial inclinations show no significant dependence on source longitude. This is consistent with low-coronal CME trajectories being governed primarily by local active-region magnetic topology, with the large-scale coronal field and heliospheric current sheet assuming a progressively dominant role at greater heliocentric distances. Incorporating DIRECD-derived propagation directions into CME arrival forecasts improves predictions of geomagnetic storm intensity, raising the correlation with observed storm strength from r = 0.58 to r = 0.70 when combined with CME speed. The DIRECD software, catalog, and all associated data products are publicly available to support space weather research.

### [4] [alpha_theta waves in the tachoclines of Sun-like stars](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21183)
*T. V. Zaqarashvili,M. Dikpati,P. A. Gilman*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 本文研究类太阳恒星塔科林（tachocline）过冲层中，发电机系数（alpha）随纬度的变化对大尺度波动线性动力学的影响，发现alpha的经向梯度会激发类Rossby波的新型大尺度波，其传播特性取决于alpha参数的垂直和纬度梯度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 以往研究关注穿透对流引起的湍流电磁力垂直梯度导致的发电机alpha效应及其产生的非传播alpha模，但对alpha系数随纬度变化如何影响塔科林过冲层中大尺度波动力学的问题尚未充分研究。

Method: 采用含发电机系数的线性磁流体动力学（MHD）方程，在简单矩形几何中建立模型。利用alpha系数的垂直梯度在感应方程中的作用与动量方程中科里奥利力类似的数学结构，分析纬度方向alpha梯度所激发的新波动模式的色散关系。

Result: alpha系数的纬度变化会激发类似旋转球面上Rossby波的新型大尺度波。波的色散特性仅取决于alpha参数的垂直和纬度梯度，因此与普通发电机波本质不同。alpha_theta波可产生前行或后退传播，取决于梯度符号；时间尺度从数百天到数十年不等。在大尺度磁场存在时，这些波与Rossby波耦合，可能导致对流能与旋转能相互转化。

Conclusion: 类太阳恒星塔科林中alpha系数的纬度变化是激发新型大尺度波的重要机制，这些波与Rossby波的耦合为理解恒星磁活动中的能量转化提供了新视角。

Abstract: Vertical gradient of mean turbulent electromotive force (expressed by the dynamo coefficient, alpha, caused by the penetrating convection into the tachoclines of Sun-like stars leads to the non-propagating alpha-mode patterns, which result in the periodic variations of magnetic field horizontal components. The aim of the paper is to study the influence of the latitudinal variation of the dynamo coefficient on the linear dynamics of large-scale waves in the overshoot layers of the tachoclines in Sun-like stars. We use the linear magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) equations with the dynamo coefficient in a simple rectangular geometry. It is shown that the vertical gradient of the alpha coefficient has the same appearance in the induction equation as the Coriolis force in the momentum equation. Therefore, the latitudinal variation of alpha parameter excites new large-scale waves similar to the Rossby waves on a rotating sphere. The dispersion properties of the waves depend only on vertical and latitudinal gradients of the alpha parameter, therefore the modes are basically different from the ordinary dynamo waves. The alpha_theta waves may have either prograde or retrograde propagation depending on the signs of the gradients. The time scales of the waves may vary from hundreds of days to tens of years in various parameters of the solar tachocline. These waves are coupled with the Rossby waves on a rotating sphere in the existence of the large scale magnetic field, which may lead to the mutual transformation of convective and rotation energies.

</details>
