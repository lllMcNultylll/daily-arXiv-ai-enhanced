*Xinyue Wang,Astrid M. Veronig,Hechao Chen,Hui Tian*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 本文报道了一个与失败的暗条爆发和受限耀斑相关的深日冕暗化事件，其主要出现在高温谱线中，由等离子体冷却而非CME驱动的质量逃逸所致。

Motivation: 传统上将日冕暗化视为日冕物质抛射（CME）的指示器，反映等离子体逃逸造成的密度耗损。然而，某些暗化事件可能并非由CME引起，理解这一点对于正确解读恒星暗化观测至关重要。该事件提供了一个挑战传统认知的案例。

Method: 利用GOES卫星和EVE仪器的\"类太阳\"全局测量观测软X射线和高温谱线（Fe XVIII和Fe XX）的强度下降；结合AIA进行的空间分辨观测定位暗化源区；使用STEREO-A排除CME质量损失；通过微分发射测量（DEM）分析等离子体温度演化。

Result: 发现软X射线强度下降超过30%，Fe XVIII（6.5 MK）和Fe XX（9.3 MK）均有可测量下降；暗化起源于活动区核心；STEREO-A未显示CME驱动质量损失的证据。DEM分析表明>5 MK的等离子体冷却到较低温度，证明等离子体冷却而非CME相关质量逃逸是高温暗化的主导机制。

Conclusion: 深层热软X射线暗化可以在没有显著CME驱动质量损失的情况下发生。这一发现表明，除常见推断的CME特征外，其他物理机制（如等离子体冷却）也可能解释未解析的恒星暗化现象，对恒星活动与CME关联性的推断具有重要启示。

Abstract: Coronal dimmings are observed as sudden and localized reductions in the extreme-ultraviolet and X-ray emission of the solar corona. Traditionally, significant dimmings of spectral lines formed at temperatures of 1-2 MK are regarded as indicators of coronal mass ejections (CMEs), reflecting the density depletion caused by plasma escaping into interplanetary space. In this Letter, we report a peculiar deep coronal dimming event predominantly observed in high-temperature spectral lines following an M8.8-class confined solar flare associated with a failed filament eruption. Sun-as-a-star measurements from the Geostationary Operational Environmental Satellite and the Extreme Ultraviolet Variability Experiment reveal intensity reductions exceeding 30% in soft X-ray (SXR) and measurable decreases in Fe XVIII (6.5 MK) and Fe XX (9.3 MK). Spatially resolved observations from the Atmospheric Imaging Assembly demonstrate that the dimming originates from the active region core, while the Solar Terrestrial Relations Observatory-A shows no evidence for CME-driven mass loss. Differential emission measure analysis reveals plasma at temperatures >5 MK cooling into lower temperatures, supporting plasma cooling as the dominant contributor to the hot-band dimming rather than CME-associated plasma escape. This event demonstrates that deep hot SXR dimmings can occur without substantial CME-driven mass loss, suggesting that alternative physical mechanisms may also account for unresolved stellar dimmings in addition to the commonly inferred CME signatures.
