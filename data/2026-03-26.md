Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 通过HST多波段光谱分析HAT-P-11恒星表面异质性及其对透射光谱的影响，发现WFC3数据支持双组分光球模型（4950K主成分+3400K冷斑覆盖26-33%），而STIS光学数据拟合不佳，反映了恒星大气模型在光学波段的局限性。

Motivation: 研究HAT-P-11这颗活跃K矮星的表面异质性（特别是星斑覆盖），评估其对高精度透射光谱观测的影响，为JWST时代恒星污染校正提供物理基础方法。

Method: 使用HST绝对定标的出凌光谱（STIS和WFC3仪器，覆盖G430L、G750L、G102、G141波段），基于NewEra PHOENIX模型进行网格化光谱反演，结合长期测光监测和色球活动指数分析。

Result: WFC3数据强烈支持双组分光球模型：4950K光球成分和~3400K冷斑成分覆盖26-33%恒星盘面；STIS光学光谱反演结果不理想，显示模型在光学波段的局限性；星斑覆盖分数与Kepler时期观测的光变幅度一致，但与TESS近期观测存在张力，同时Ca II H&K指数下降20%，表明恒星活动存在长期变化。

Conclusion: HAT-P-11存在相对宁静的活动相位，为大气表征提供了更有利的观测窗口；多历元空间光谱为JWST时代高精度透射光谱的恒星污染校正提供了物理基础途径。

Abstract: HAT-P-11 is a well-studied, active K dwarf hosting an eccentric, misaligned transiting sub-Neptune. As part of the HST Stellar Treasure Trove program (HST-AR-17551), we analyze absolutely calibrated out-of-transit \HST{} spectra from \texttt{STIS} and \texttt{WFC3} across the \textsc{G430L}, \textsc{G750L}, \textsc{G102}, and \textsc{G141} bandpasses to constrain the surface heterogeneity of HAT-P-11 and its impact on transmission spectroscopy. Grid-based spectral retrievals using NewEra \texttt{PHOENIX} models robustly favor two-component photospheres in the \texttt{WFC3} G102 and G141 data, with a ${\sim}4950$\,K photospheric component and a cooler ($\sim$3400\,K) component covering 26{--}33\% of the stellar disk. By contrast, retrievals on the \texttt{STIS} optical spectra do not yield a satisfactory fit, reflecting current limitations of stellar atmosphere models in the optical regime compared to the \HST{} observational precision. We contextualize these results using long-term photometric monitoring and chromospheric activity indices. The inferred high spot covering fractions are broadly consistent with the elevated photometric variability observed during the \textit{Kepler} era ($f_{\rm spot}$$\sim$10--20\%) but are in tension with the much lower rotational amplitudes observed from TESS in the mid 2020s ($f_{\rm spot}$$\sim$1--10\%). This secular decline in variability is mirrored by a $\sim$20\% decrease in the Ca\,\textsc{ii} H\&K index. These results imply that HAT-P-11 undergoes comparatively quiescent phases that offer more favorable windows for atmospheric characterization, which serendipitously coincided with some of the recent JWST observations. More generally, our study demonstrates that multi-epoch, space-based stellar spectra provides a physically grounded pathway for mitigating stellar contamination in high-precision transmission spectra in the JWST era.
