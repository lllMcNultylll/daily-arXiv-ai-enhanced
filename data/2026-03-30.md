### [1] [Toward a Comprehensive Grid of Cepheid Models with MESA. III. Evolutionary and Pulsation Relations for Models with Core and Envelope Overshooting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26111)
*R. Smolec,O. Ziółkowska,R. Singh Rathour,V. Hocdé,P. Wielgórski*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 该研究使用MESA计算了2-8M⊙的经典造父变星演化轨迹，覆盖[Fe/H]=-1.0到+0.2的金属丰度范围，分析了对流超射和质量损失对演化及脉动性质的影响，提供了不稳定带边界、周期-光度关系等关键参数。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究经典造父变星的演化与脉动特性，特别是对流超射、质量损失和金属丰度对不稳定带边界、周期-光度关系等关键参数的影响，以更好地理解这些重要距离指示器的物理机制。

Method: 使用MESA（Modules for Experiments in Stellar Astrophysics）计算2-8M⊙恒星的演化轨迹，覆盖[Fe/H]=-1.0到+0.2的金属丰度范围。采用RSP（Radial Stellar Pulsation）模块进行线性脉动分析。研究包含对流超射（主序核和对流包层）和质量损失的影响。

Result: 提供了不稳定带边界、穿越时间、周期变化率等参数。建立了周期-光度、质量-光度、周期-半径和周期-年龄关系，发现周期-光度关系的金属丰度效应γ≈-0.20 mag dex⁻¹，与近期观测研究一致。模型与大多数观测数据吻合良好，但在重现小麦哲伦云短周期造父变星和解决造父变星质量差异方面仍面临挑战。

Conclusion: 该研究成功建立了覆盖宽金属丰度范围的经典造父变星演化模型，提供了重要的理论关系。周期-光度关系的金属丰度效应与观测一致，但短周期造父变星和质量差异问题仍需进一步研究。这些结果为造父变星作为距离指示器和恒星演化研究提供了重要理论支持。

Abstract: Evolutionary tracks for 2-8M$_\odot$ models, covering a [Fe/H]=$-$1.0 ($Z=0.0014$) to [Fe/H]=+0.2 ($Z=0.02$) metallicity range are computed with Modules for Experiments in Stellar Astrophysics, MESA, to investigate evolutionary and pulsation properties of classical, fundamental mode Cepheids. We examine in detail the effects of convective overshooting from the Main Sequence core, as well as from the convective envelope on the Red Giant Branch. Mass loss is also included in a few model sets. Linear pulsation properties are derived consistently with a module of MESA, Radial Stellar Pulsation, RSP. We provide edges of the classical Instability Strip, as well as ages, crossing times through the Instability Strip and period change rates. Period-Luminosity, Mass-Luminosity, Period-Radius and Period-Age relations are provided, both in analytical and tabular form. Their dependence on metallicity, crossing number and overshooting parameters are investigated. Qualitative comparisons with classical Cepheids in the Milky Way and Magellanic Clouds as well as other theoretical relations are presented. We find satisfactory agreement for most of the observables and good match with other theoretical work, however reproducing short-period Cepheids in the Small Magellanic Cloud as well as Cepheid mass discrepancy pose a challenge for the presented models. Considering metallicity effect of the Period-Luminosity relation, we find $γ\approx -0.20$ mag dex$^{-1}$, nearly independent on photometric pass band and in good agreement with recent observational studies. The magnitude of this effect depends on the underlying mass-luminosity relation, being stronger for relations that predict higher luminosities at a given mass.

### [2] [Direct evidence for magnetohydrodynamic disk winds driving rotating outflows in protostar HOPS 358](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26120)
*Chul-Hwan Kim,Jeong-Eun Lee,Doug Johnstone,Gregory J. Herczeg,Chin-Fei Lee,Logan Francis,Patrick D. Sheehan*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: ALMA观测揭示HOPS 358原恒星存在旋转嵌套外流结构，为磁流体动力学盘风提供了直接观测证据，证明其在行星形成区有效提取角动量。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 角动量移除是恒星和行星形成的基本要求，但其机制仍存在争议。磁流体动力学盘风提供了一种有前景的解决方案，特别是在磁旋转湍流较弱的区域，需要直接观测证据来验证这一机制。

Method: 使用高分辨率阿塔卡马大型毫米/亚毫米波阵列（ALMA）观测0级原恒星HOPS 358，通过H2CO、SO和CH3OH发射线追踪旋转嵌套外流结构，分析其运动学特征。

Result: 观测发现外流结构保留了盘的旋转方向并与盘轴对齐，为磁驱动盘风提供了直接证据。测得无量纲磁杠杆臂约为2.3，约束风发射区域位于行星形成区半径10-18天文单位范围内。

Conclusion: 磁流体动力学盘风在深埋藏阶段就已运行，能有效提取角动量并塑造盘演化，为行星形成建立了初始条件。这解决了角动量移除的关键问题，对理解恒星和行星形成过程具有重要意义。

Abstract: Angular momentum removal is a fundamental requirement for star and planet formation, yet the mechanisms driving this process remain debated. Magnetohydrodynamic disk winds, launched along magnetic field lines from extended disk regions, offer a promising solution, particularly in regions where magnetorotational turbulence is weak. Here we present high-resolution Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array observations of the Class 0 protostar HOPS 358, revealing a rotating, nested outflow structure traced by H2CO, SO, and CH3OH emission. The outflow preserves the disk's rotational sense and is aligned with the disk axis, providing direct observational evidence for a magnetically launched disk wind. From the measured kinematics, we derive a dimensionless magnetic lever arm of approximately 2.3 and constrain the wind-launching region to radii of 10-18 astronomical units within the planet-forming zone. These results demonstrate that magnetohydrodynamic disk winds operate during the deeply embedded phase, efficiently extracting angular momentum while shaping disk evolution and establishing initial conditions for planet formation.
