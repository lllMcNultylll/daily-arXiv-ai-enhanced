Method: 使用MESA r24.08.01恒星演化程序，从预主序前阶段到白矮星冷却曲线进行完整模拟，包括139次热脉动和AGB后阶段，并比较不同质量损失率方案和相分离效应的影响。

Result: 获得1.313太阳质量的缺氢超重白矮星，元素质量分数为：47.7% O16、39.7% Ne20、4.2% Mg24、3.3% Na23、0.386% C12（总碳质量5×10^-3太阳质量），外层为1.5×10^-5太阳质量氦层。相分离仅导致16Myr的冷却延迟。

Conclusion: 这是首个计算了TP-SAGB和AGB后完整阶段的超重白矮星演化序列，也是已发表的最完整大质量白矮星演化模型，为理解超重白矮星的形成和性质提供了重要基准。

Abstract: We study the full evolution of a 1.313 Msun white dwarf star that descended from a 9 Msun main-sequence progenitor with an initial metallicity of Z=0.02. Using MESA r24.08.01, we calculate its entire evolution from pre-ZAMS to the WD cooling curve, including both the evolution through 139 thermal pulses and the post-AGB phase. The resulting remnant is an ultramassive H-deficient WD, for which the composition, in mass fraction, is 47.7% O16, 39.7% Ne20, 4.2% Mg24, 3.3% Na23 and 0.386% C12 -- corresponding to a total mass of 5 x 10^-3 Msun of C --, surrounded by a 1.5 x 10^-5 Msun He layer. We also investigate the effects of fully suppressing the TP-SAGB stage by adopting a high mass-loss rate only after the second dredge-up, and find only minor differences in the final mass and composition. In addition, we calculate models with and without phase separation during the WD stage, estimating a cooling delay of only 16 Myr. This is the first ultramassive white dwarf sequence for which both the TP-SAGB and post-AGB stages are calculated and, to our knowledge, the most massive WD model from complete evolution for which cooling times and detailed abundance profiles are published
