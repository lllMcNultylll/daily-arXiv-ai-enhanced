### [1] [Examining the Effects of Magnetic Field Extrapolation on MHD Flare Simulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04557)
*W. Bate,M. Gordovskyy,A. Prasad,A. S. Brun,A. Strugarek,M. V. Sieyra,P. Browning,S. Inoue,K. Matsumoto,A. Roddanavar*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 对比研究两种磁流体动力学太阳耀斑模拟：传统非线性无力场外推与非无力场外推，后者包含等离子体压力和重力，结果显示非无力场模型释放更多磁能且与观测更一致。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统太阳耀斑模拟基于非线性无力场外推，但该假设忽略了等离子体力和重力，可能限制可用自由磁能。本研究旨在通过对比无力场与非无力场初始磁场配置，评估初始磁场假设对耀斑动力学和能量学的影响。

Method: 对2011年9月6日NOAA活动区11283的X2.1级耀斑进行三维电阻磁流体动力学模拟对比。两个模拟仅在初始磁场配置上不同：一个基于传统非线性无力场外推，另一个采用自洽包含等离子体压力和重力的非无力场外推。两者在相同分层大气中使用相同数值框架演化，计算合成极紫外94Å通道发射并与SDO观测对比。

Result: 非无力场模型经历更广泛的磁重联，释放约4.4×10³¹ erg磁能，是无力场模型（约2.3×10³¹ erg）的两倍，更接近X级耀斑预期能量预算。非无力场模型产生的94Å通道合成发射更亮、空间分布更广，与观测的耀斑形态和光变曲线更相似。

Conclusion: 构建耀斑前日冕磁场的假设显著影响耀斑能量学和可观测特征。非无力场外推为更真实的数据约束耀斑建模提供了有前景的途径。

Abstract: Solar flare simulations are commonly initialised using non-linear force free field (NLFF) extrapolations derived from photospheric vector magnetograms. However, the force free assumption neglects plasma forces and may limit the available free magnetic energy. In this work, we perform a controlled comparison of two three-dimensional resistive magnetohydrodynamic simulations of the X2.1-class flare that occurred on 2011 September 06 in NOAA Active Region 11283. The simulations differ only in their initial magnetic configuration: one is based on a conventional NLFF extrapolation, while the other employs a non-force free extrapolation that self-consistently incorporates plasma pressure and gravity. Both models are evolved in an identical stratified atmosphere using the same numerical framework, enabling direct assessment of how the initial magnetic assumptions influence flare dynamics and energetics.
  We find that the non-force free model undergoes more extensive magnetic restructuring and releases approximately twice as much magnetic energy ($\approx4.4 \times 10^{31}$ erg) as the NLFF case ($\approx2.3 \times 10^{31}$ erg), bringing the energy budget into closer agreement with expectations for X-class flares. Synthetic extreme ultraviolet emission in the 94A channel is computed for both simulations and compared with observations from the Solar Dynamics Observatory. The non-force free model produces a brighter and more spatially extended emission structure that more closely resembles the observed flare morphology and light curve. These results demonstrate that assumptions made in constructing the pre-flare coronal magnetic field can significantly affect flare energetics and observable signatures, and suggest that non-force free extrapolations provide a promising pathway toward more realistic data-constrained flare modelling.

### [2] [Stellar contents and Star Formation in IRAS 18456-0223](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05224)
*Nilesh Pandey,U. S. Kamath*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 利用多波段数据研究IRAS 18456-0223恒星形成区的年轻恒星天体，识别89个YSOs，分析其物理性质、空间分布和变异性，并揭示冷丝状结构中的柱密度峰值。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究IRAS 18456-0223恒星形成区中年轻恒星天体的性质、空间分布和演化状态，以理解该区域恒星形成的物理过程和结构特征。

Method: 结合光学（Gaia DR3）和红外（2MASS、UKIDSS、Spitzer、WISE、Herschel）多波段数据，辅以光学光谱观测；基于红外特性识别YSOs，通过SED拟合获取物理参数，使用最小生成树分析空间分布，比较UKIDSS和2MASS数据研究变异性，构建Herschel柱密度和温度图。

Result: 识别出89个YSOs（80个Class II，9个Class I），质量范围0.1-7.2 M⊙，年龄可达4 Myr；形成约600 pc距离处的星团，径向尺度约0.5 pc，平均面密度~60 pc⁻²；部分YSOs显示变异性；Herschel图揭示多个柱密度峰值（N_H₂ ~ 10²² cm⁻²）嵌入冷丝状结构（T_d ~ 10-13 K）；三个亮源光谱显示A-K光谱型，其中一个具有Li I 6707 Å线证实年轻性。

Conclusion: IRAS 18456-0223区域存在活跃的恒星形成活动，YSOs呈现空间聚集特征，冷丝状结构中存在高柱密度区域，为恒星形成提供了物质基础，多波段分析有效揭示了该区域的恒星形成状态和物理环境。

Abstract: We use various analytical techniques to study Young Stellar Objects (YSOs) in an area of approximately $10' \times 10'$ in the IRAS 18456-0223 star-forming region. We use archival optical (Gaia DR3) and infrared (2MASS, UKIDSS, Spitzer, WISE, and Herschel) data, along with our optical spectroscopy of three bright stars for this purpose. We identify 89 YSOs (80 Class II and 9 Class I) based on their infrared properties. Our multiwavelength SED fits of selected YSOs show that they have masses $\sim 0.1$--$7.2$ $M_\odot$ and are up to $4$ Myr old. Our Minimum Spanning Tree (MST) analysis shows that these YSOs, situated at around 600 pc, form clusters with radial extents of order 0.5 pc and mean surface densities of $\sim 60$ pc$^{-2}$. We compare UKIDSS and 2MASS data of the YSOs and find that some of them show variability. We construct maps based on Herschel data which reveal multiple column density peaks ($N_{\rm H_2} \sim 10^{22}$ cm$^{-2}$) embedded in cold ($T_d \sim 10$--$13$ K) filaments. Our near-infrared extinction map exhibits several high-$A_V$ peaks, some of which coincide with the sub-mm column density maxima. Using our optical spectra of three bright sources, we show that they are of A--K spectral type. One star shows the Li I 6707 Å line, indicating its youth.

### [3] [HD 188101: A Spotted B Star with Abundance Anomalies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05307)
*R. M. Bayazitov,L. I. Mashonkina,Yu. V. Pakhomov,I. A. Yakunin*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 通过光谱和测光观测确定了弱磁场恒星HD 188101的基本参数，发现其属于化学特殊He-弱SiTiSr星类型，并揭示了多种元素丰度异常和谱线变化特征。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究HD 188101这颗研究较少的弱磁场恒星，确定其基本物理参数和化学丰度特征，探究其化学特殊性和变异性。

Method: 结合光谱观测和测光观测（包括Kepler数据），采用非局部热动平衡修正的方法，测定恒星有效温度、表面重力及He、C、O、Mg、Si、Ti、Sr等元素的丰度。

Result: 确定HD 188101为B9型主序星（Teff=14200±990 K，log g=3.70±0.16）；发现Si、Ti、Sr相对太阳丰度过丰，He丰度略低于太阳但差异在误差范围内；观测到He I、Mg II、Si II、Si III、Ti II、Fe II谱线的吸收变化，且不同He I和Mg II谱线在同一观测相位给出不同丰度。

Conclusion: HD 188101属于化学特殊He-弱SiTiSr星群，具有典型的B9主序星参数但表现出明显的化学丰度异常和谱线变异性，这些特征可能与其弱磁场和化学特殊性质相关。

Abstract: Based on spectroscopic and photometric observations, we have determined the fundamental parameters of the poorly studied star HD 188101 with a weak magnetic field. Its effective temperature $T_{\rm eff} = 14200 \pm 990$ K and surface gravity log g = 3.70 $\pm$ 0.16 are typical for main-sequence B9 stars. The He, C, O, Mg, Si, Ti, and Sr abundances have been determined by taking into account the departures from local thermodynamic equilibrium. Overabundances of Si, Ti, and Sr relative to their solar abundances have been revealed. The He abundance is lower than the solar one, but the difference is within the error limits. In addition to the photometric variability known from Kepler data, we have found changes in absorption for He I, Mg II, Si II, Si III, Ti II, and Fe II lines, with different He I and Mg II lines giving different abundances for the same phase of observations. The star HD 188101 is shown to belong to the group of chemically peculiar He-weak SiTiSr stars.

### [4] [TILARA: Template-Independent Line-by-line Algorithm for Radial velocity Analysis. I. Description of the code and application on a Sun-like star](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05445)
*C. San Nicolas Martinez,N. C. Santos,V. Adibekyan,K. Al Moulla,A. M. Silva,S. G. Sousa*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: TILARA是一种模板独立、逐线提取径向速度的代码，无需依赖参考光谱模板，通过高斯拟合自动测量吸收线中心来计算径向速度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统径向速度提取方法（交叉相关和模板匹配）依赖参考光谱模板，在数据可变、受污染或稀疏采样时会产生偏差。逐线方法虽然提供了替代方案，但通常仍需要模板创建，存在固有局限性。

Method: TILARA使用精心挑选的吸收线列表作为参考，通过ARES进行高斯拟合自动测量线中心。基于参考线列表和目标恒星光谱中测量的线，计算径向速度，并通过sigma-clipping或降权方法应用可配置的异常值剔除。

Result: 在520个ESPRESSO对类太阳恒星HD 102365的观测中测试了不同配置。TILARA在sigma-clipping和降权模式下，产生的径向速度时间序列标准差和误差条与现有不同方法得到的结果相似。

Conclusion: TILARA提供了一种有效的模板独立径向速度提取方法，在光谱模板构建不可行的情况下仍能有效工作，性能与现有方法相当，具有处理不同恒星光谱类型和仪器的灵活性。

Abstract: Precise radial velocities (RVs) are commonly derived through cross-correlation or template-matching methods, both of which rely on a reference spectrum that can introduce biases when the data are variable, contaminated, or sparsely sampled. Line-by-line methods offer an alternative way to compute RVs but generally still rely on template creation and therefore share its inherent limitations. We introduce TILARA, a template-independent, line-by-line RV extraction code designed to allow us to derive line-by-line RVs and to operate effectively even when spectral template construction is not recommended. While originally motivated by future PoET disk-resolved solar observations, TILARA has been built with the flexibility to work with different stellar spectral types and instruments. A curated list of individual absorption lines is used as a reference to automatically measure line centers with via Gaussian fitting with ARES. Then, using the reference lines list, and the lines measured with ARES on the spectra of the target star, TILARA computes the RVs and applies configurable outlier rejection through sigma-clipping or down-weighting methods. We tested different configurations of the code, RV uncertainty estimation methods, and line selection criteria. The code was applied to 520 ESPRESSO observations of the Sun-like star HD 102365 to evaluate its performance. TILARA was then tested against other RV extraction methods. Both in its sigma-clipping and its down-weighting mode, TILARA provided resulting RV time-series with similar standard deviation and error bars as the ones derived using existing methods that follow different approaches.

</details>
