Abstract: Radial velocity (RV) is crucial for stellar kinematics and Galactic archaeology. The Large Sky Area Multi-Object Fiber Spectroscopic Telescope (LAMOST) has obtained over ten million low-resolution spectra ($R \sim 1800$), yielding RVs for millions of stars, but these suffer from (1) wavelength-dependent inconsistencies (relative shifts between spectral segments) and (2) global zero-point offsets (uniform shifts of entire spectra).
