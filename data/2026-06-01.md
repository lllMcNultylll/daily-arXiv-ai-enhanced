### [1] [Harmonics as a Hidden Window into the Turbulent Convective Envelope of non-Blazhko RRab Stars](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31528)
*Jia-Shu Niu*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 本文发现非Blazhko RRab型变星（传统认为脉动稳定）中存在谐波振幅和频率变化现象，挑战了谐波仅是傅里叶假象的传统观点，并提出这些谐波结构源于恒星对流包层的湍流活动，为研究对流-脉动相互作用提供了新窗口。

Motivation: 传统上，脉动星的谐波被视为完全由基模决定的傅里叶假象，但近期大振幅δ Scuti星中发现了非谐调谐波（振幅和频率变化与基模无关）。本文旨在检验这一现象的普适性，研究传统认为脉动稳定的非Blazhko RRab星中是否存在类似谐波变异性。

Method: 利用Kepler卫星短节奏测光数据，分析非Blazhko RRab星的频率谱，识别由基模谐波形成的凸起结构，并测量这些谐波在凸起结构起始和尾部的振幅和频率变化。

Result: 在非Blazhko RRab星中确实发现了由基模谐波形成的凸起结构，且谐波展现出显著的振幅和频率变化，这些变化与基模无关，集中在凸起结构的起始和尾部区域。

Conclusion: 谐波凸起结构及其内在变异性可能源于恒星湍流对流包层，为追踪对流-脉动相互作用中的能量注入和湍流随机扰动提供了潜在窗口，但需详细模型验证。

Abstract: Harmonics in pulsating stars have traditionally been regarded as mere Fourier artifacts, fully determined by their parent mode. Recent observations of high-amplitude $δ$ Scuti stars, however, have revealed the existence of disharmonized harmonics, which exhibit amplitude and frequency variations uncorrelated with their parent pulsation mode. Here we test the universality of this phenomenon by examining a class of stars long considered pulsationally stable: non-Blazhko RRab stars. Using short-cadence {\it Kepler} photometric data, we not only identify a distinct hump structure formed by harmonics of the primary pulsation mode in their frequency spectra, but also find significant amplitude and frequency variations associated with the harmonics around the onset and in the tail of the hump structure. These findings are consistent with the interpretation that the hump structure originates from the turbulent convective envelope of the star. Interestingly, several important phenomena can be understood within this framework as a working hypothesis. Thus, the hump structure of harmonics and their intrinsic variability could constitute a hidden window into the stellar convective envelope, potentially tracing energy injection at the convection-pulsation interaction and stochastic perturbations from turbulent convection -- although detailed modeling is required to confirm this interpretation.
