### [1] [EPISODE IV: Ice Inventory in the Envelope of EC 53](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08769)
*Jaeyeong Kim,Jeong-Eun Lee,Chul-Hwan Kim,Seokho Lee,Giseon Baek,Seonjae Lee,Yao-Lun Yang,Yuri Aikawa,Gregory J. Herczeg,Doug Johnstone,Joel D. Green,Woong-Seob Jeong*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 利用JWST观测年轻原恒星EC 53在宁静期和爆发期的红外光谱，通过扣除硅酸盐吸收来分离冰吸收特征，分析其冰成分组成及丰度变化


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究周期性吸积爆发对原恒星包层中冰的物理和化学状态的影响，了解爆发事件中冰成分的演化过程

Method: 使用JWST NIRSpec IFU和MIRI MRS获取1.6-28μm光谱；通过专用连续谱拟合程序扣除硅酸盐吸收；用实验室纯冰和H2O混合冰谱线匹配分解主要冰成分

Result: EC 53中主要冰成分（H2O、NH3、CO2、CH3OH、CO、CH4）相对丰度显著高于其他原恒星；两个相位间所有冰吸收带均无显著变化；检测到OCN-和NH4+离子特征

Conclusion: EC 53的中等强度、短周期吸积爆发不会显著改变其包层中冰的物理或化学状态，其化学丰富、热宁静的冰库为研究间歇性吸积下的冰演化提供了基准

Abstract: We present the 1.6$-$28 $μ$m spectra of the young protostar EC 53, obtained with JWST NIRSpec IFU and MIRI MRS during the quiescent and burst phases of its periodic brightness variations. To isolate ice absorption features, we modeled and removed the mid-infrared silicate dust absorption using a dedicated continuum-fitting procedure. In the optical depth spectrum, we first fit the broad H$_2$O ice features and then decomposed the major ice components, including NH$_3$, CO$_2$, CH$_3$OH, CO, and CH$_4$, by matching laboratory profiles for both pure and H$_2$O-mixed ices. The 4.62 $μ$m and 6.85 $μ$m bands are attributed to OCN$^-$ and NH$_4^+$ ions, respectively. Minor or tentative contributions from complex species (HCOOH, H$_2$CO, CH$_3$COOH, CH$_3$CHO, CH$_3$CH$_2$OH, and NH$_2$CHO) are also considered to our global ice analysis. The silicate-corrected spectra reveal no measurable change in any ice absorption band between the two phases, indicating that moderate and short-period accretion bursts in EC~53 do not significantly alter the physical or chemical state of the ices within its envelope. The derived abundances of these major species relative to H$_2$O significantly exceed the values typically observed toward other embedded protostars. Finally, we place the ice inventory of EC~53 in the context of other protostellar systems observed with JWST, highlighting that its chemically rich, thermally quiescent ice reservoir provides a benchmark for studying ice evolution under episodic accretion.

### [2] [Validating a Non-conventional Method for Expansion of Coronal Mass Ejections (CMEs) and Investigating the Evolution of a CME Substructures Using Solar Orbiter and Wind Observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08842)
*Anjali Agarwal,Wageesh Mishra,Mathew J. Owens,Tanja Amerstorfer*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 验证CAAP方法用于估计日冕物质抛射瞬时膨胀速度的有效性，利用SolO和Wind航天器的径向对齐配置，通过直接测量与CAAP估计值对比，并分析了CME子结构的时空演化特征。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 验证新提出的CAAP方法在仅有单点原位观测时估计CME瞬时膨胀速度的可靠性，利用罕见的航天器径向对齐配置提供直接测量验证机会。

Method: 利用SolO和Wind航天器在2021年11月3-5日的径向对齐配置（0.13 AU径向分离，2.3度角分离），同时观测CME磁云的中心（Wind）和尾缘（SolO），直接测量瞬时膨胀速度并与CAAP方法估计值对比。使用最小方差分析（MVA）估计磁云轴倾角，追踪CME子结构（激波、鞘层、磁云）的时空演化。

Result: CAAP方法估计的瞬时膨胀速度与直接测量结果一致，验证了该方法有效性。发现MVA估计的低倾角磁云轴与视觉检查显示的高倾角ENW型磁云轴存在差异。观察到磁云从SolO到Wind传播过程中磁通量明显增加，违反磁通守恒。激波在Wind处意外增强，鞘层厚度基本保持不变，可能由于高速流背压压缩和环境太阳风变异性导致磁云加速。

Conclusion: CAAP方法适用于估计CME瞬时膨胀速度，验证了其在单点观测条件下的可靠性。CME子结构的时空演化对空间天气研究至关重要，观测到的磁通量不守恒和激波增强现象揭示了CME传播过程中的复杂动力学过程。

Abstract: We present a validation of our recently proposed non-conventional method, Constant Acceleration Accounted Perspective (CAAP), for estimating the instantaneous expansion speed of coronal mass ejection (CMEs), even when only single-point in situ observations are available. This validation is enabled by the radial alignment of SolO and Wind spacecraft (0.13 AU radial and 2.3 deg angular separation), providing simultaneous observations of the center (at Wind) and trailing edge (at SolO) of a CME associated magnetic cloud (MC) during 3-5 November 2021, allowing a direct measurement of its instantaneous expansion speed. These measurements are compared with CAAP-derived instantaneous expansion speed estimates at both spacecraft. The favorable spacecraft configuration also enables tracking the temporal evolution of CME substructures, including the shock, sheath, and MC. A discrepancy is noted between the low-inclination MC axis estimated from minimum variance analysis (MVA) and the highly inclined ENW-type MC axis suggested by visual inspection of in situ measurements. We also observe an apparent increase in the magnetic flux within the MC from SolO to Wind, indicating a noticeable deviation from magnetic flux conservation. During the CME's propagation from SolO to Wind, the shock becomes unexpectedly stronger at Wind, while the sheath thickness remains nearly the same, likely due to MC acceleration from back compression by a high-speed stream and ambient solar wind variability. Our results demonstrate the applicability of the CAAP method and the importance of accounting for temporal evolution in CME substructures for space weather studies.

### [3] [A Convolutional Neural Network-Derived Catalog of Solar Flares from Soft X-Ray Observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08928)
*Nastaran Farhang,Michael. S. Wheatland,Andrew Melatos*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 使用卷积神经网络(CNN)基于GOES高分辨率软X射线数据构建新的太阳耀斑目录，检测到比传统GOES目录多7倍的耀斑候选事件，揭示了更陡的幂律分布和小耀斑的更高灵敏度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统GOES耀斑目录可能遗漏大量小耀斑事件，特别是在高流量期间存在计数不足问题，需要更灵敏的检测方法来获得完整的耀斑统计特性。

Method: 使用卷积神经网络(CNN)分析1秒高分辨率GOES软X射线数据，训练网络识别耀斑上升阶段；采用贝叶斯推断量化每个候选事件为真阳性的概率；通过峰值流量、上升时间和与已知事件的时序重合度进行验证。

Result: 在2018-2025年间检测到111,580个耀斑候选，远多于GOES目录的14,612个；CNN目录显示更陡的原始峰值流量幂律指数(-2.59 vs -2.25)，对小耀斑更敏感；背景修正后指数分别为-1.97和-2.05；将小流量端幂律分布扩展了一个数量级。

Conclusion: CNN耀斑目录提供了更完整一致的太阳耀斑统计基础，揭示了先前报道的耀斑大小与等待时间相关性受计数不足影响；新目录支持分段泊松过程模型，为太阳耀斑统计研究提供了改进工具。

Abstract: A convolutional neural network (CNN) is used to construct a new catalog for solar flares based on high resolution (1-s cadence) Geostationary Operational Environmental Satellites (GOES) soft X-ray data. The CNN is trained to identify flare rise episodes. From 1 January 2018 to 22 August 2025, the algorithm detects 111,580 flare candidates, compared with 14,612 events in the corresponding GOES catalog. For each candidate, the probability of being a true positive is quantified by Bayesian inference based on the peak flux, rise time, and temporal coincidence with cataloged events where available. The flare size and waiting-time distributions are studied and compared with the GOES catalog. The CNN catalog shows a steeper power-law index for raw peak fluxes (-2.59 -\+ 0.02) than GOES (-2.25 -\+ 0.04), indicating the CNN's higher sensitivity to small events. After background correction, the indices are -1.97 -\+ 0.02 (CNN) and -2.05 -\+ 0.04 (GOES). The CNN catalog extends the power-law distribution of flare peak fluxes by one order of magnitude at the small-flux end compared with the GOES background-subtracted catalog. A Bayesian blocks analysis of the waiting-time distributions from the GOES and CNN catalogs indicates broad consistency with a piecewise Poisson process. We find that previously reported correlations between flare sizes and waiting times are significantly influenced by obscuration, that is, under-counting weaker or overlapping flares during periods of elevated flux. The new CNN catalog provides a foundation for complete and consistent studies of solar flare statistics.
