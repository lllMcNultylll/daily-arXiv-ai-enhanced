### [1] [An unresolved extreme warm infrared excess toward a nearby low-mass star](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12034)
*Sahin Torlakcik*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 本文报告了一颗近邻低质量恒星 Gaia DR3 2981818948336660224 在中红外波段存在显著热过剩，但该过剩的起源尚未确定。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在 Gaia-2MASS-WISE 联合搜寻中，发现该星具有明显的暖中红外（W3）过剩。研究动机在于确认该 W3 辐射是否真正与目标星成协，并探讨其可能的物理来源（如星周尘埃导致的极端暖碎屑盘）。

Method: 该星距约 127 pc，AllWISE W3 信噪比为 33.1。研究使用全部 16 次低温曝光（覆盖 1.26 d）并逐一移除单次曝光检验信号稳健性；将 W3 辐射中心与恒星传播位置比较，检验 6.1 角秒外已知邻近源能否重现其形态；并进行名义场景拟合以分解 W3 通量来源。

Result: W3 信号在所有可用曝光中均可恢复，移除任一单次曝光仍保持；W3 辐射中心接近目标星传播位置，已知 6.1 角秒邻源无法重现该形态；名义拟合将 97-98% 的 W3 通量归位于目标星位置。若假设成协并保留较弱的 W4 探测，过剩可用约 300-350 K 组分描述，L_IR/L_star ~ 0.022。

Conclusion: 结果强支持 W3 过剩与目标星成协，但模板不确定性和一个几乎重合的隐藏源使分解无法定论。若源于星周尘埃，则属于无青年证据的矮星周围的极端暖碎屑情形，其起源仍未解决。该目标因可重复的过剩和紧密空间成协，适合用亚角秒中红外成像与光谱进一步检验暖尘埃解释。

Abstract: Gaia DR3 2981818948336660224 is a nearby low-mass star with a substantial warm mid-infrared excess. Identified in a Gaia-2MASS-WISE search, it lies at approximately 127 pc and has an AllWISE W3 signal-to-noise ratio of 33.1. We recover the emission in all 16 available cryogenic exposures over 1.26 d; removing any one exposure leaves the signal intact. The W3 emission is centred close to the propagated stellar position, and the known neighbour 6.1 arcsec away fails to reproduce its morphology. Nominal scene fits place 97-98 per cent of the W3 flux at the target position. This provides strong spatial support for association, although template uncertainty and a nearly coincident hidden source prevent a definitive decomposition. The excess can be described by a roughly 300-350 K component with L_IR/L_star ~ 0.022, assuming stellar association and retaining the weaker W4 detection. If produced by circumstellar dust, it falls in the extreme warm-debris regime around a dwarf with no established evidence of youth. Its origin remains unresolved. The repeatable excess and close spatial association make this a useful target for subarcsecond MIR imaging and spectroscopy to test the warm-dust interpretation.

### [2] [Superoutbursts and Superhumps of Cataclysmic Variables observed with TESS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12461)
*Qi-Bin Sun,Sheng-Bang Qian,Li-Ying Zhu,Qin-Mei Li,Wen-Ping Liao,Fu-Xing Li,Wen-Xu Lin,Min-Yu Li,Ping Li,Fang-Zhou Yan,Zi-Mo Li,Tao Wang,Mao-Han Zhang*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 基于TESS对30个SU UMa型矮新星（37次超爆发）的系统分析，在29个系统中探测到相干superhump信号（含5个首次探测），测定或改进13个天体的轨道周期，揭示标准A-B-C三阶段演化并发现两个违反经典进动理论的异常系统。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: SU UMa型激变变星的superoutburst与superhump是其标志性特征，但受地基测光监测限制，superhump演化行为的完整多样性尚未得到充分约束。

Method: 利用TESS高节奏测光数据，对30个SU UMa型矮新星的37次超爆发事件进行系统分析，识别相干superhump信号，拟合周期演化阶段（A-B-C），并由Stage A周期推导线质量比。

Result: 29个系统探测到相干superhump（5个首次），13个天体轨道周期得以测定或改进；高节奏光变曲线清晰分辨标准三阶段演化；由Stage A周期得到18个系统的动力学质量比，与既有激变变星演化轨迹一致；Stage B周期导数与地基结果范围相符，显著正漂移集中于短周期、低质量比系统；重复超爆发呈现一致演化模式。RZ LMi出现与经典进动理论不符的反向阶段序列，ASASSN-14kj无阶段演化但存在相干长周期调制；3个系统在正常爆发期间持续存在superhump。

Conclusion: superhump形态是内禀且可重复的性质；正常爆发期持续superhump表明偏心率盘结构可存续于母超爆发之后，揭示热吸积与潮汐偏心率周期的解耦；空间测光在探测吸积盘动力学及检验进动模型方面具有显著优势。

Abstract: Superoutbursts and superhumps are characteristic signatures of SU UMa-type cataclysmic variables, yet the full diversity of superhump evolutionary behaviour remains poorly constrained owing to the limitations of ground-based photometric monitoring. Here we report a systematic analysis of 30 SU UMa-type dwarf novae observed with the Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite, covering 37 superoutburst events. We detect coherent superhump signals in 29 systems-including five first-time detections-and determine or refine orbital periods for 13 objects. The high-cadence light curves clearly resolve the canonical three-stage (A-B-C) superhump period evolution. Using Stage A periods, we derive dynamical mass ratios for 18 systems, consistent with established cataclysmic variable evolutionary tracks. The measured Stage B period derivatives match the range of values from ground-based campaigns, with prominent positive drifts concentrated in short-period, low-mass-ratio systems. Repeated superoutbursts yield consistent evolutionary patterns, demonstrating that superhump morphology is an intrinsic, repeatable property. Two systems break the standard template: RZ LMi shows an inverted stage sequence inconsistent with classical precession theory, while ASASSN-14kj exhibits no stage evolution but a coherent long-period modulation. Persistent superhumps during normal outbursts in three systems provide direct evidence that eccentric disk structures can survive beyond their parent superoutbursts, revealing a decoupling between thermal accretion and tidal eccentricity cycles. These results demonstrate the power of space-borne photometry for probing accretion disk dynamics and testing precession models across the cataclysmic variable parameter space.
