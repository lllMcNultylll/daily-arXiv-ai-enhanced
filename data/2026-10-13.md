### [1] [Formation Dynamics of a Rotating Solar Spicule-like Event and Associated Energy-Mass Transport](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13173)
*Liufan Gong,Lei Ni,Guanchong Cheng,Jiajia Liu,Quan Xie,Robert Erdélyi*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 通过3D辐射磁流体力学模拟，揭示正极性开放磁场中与磁龙卷相关的旋转型针状体事件由光球涡旋驱动，磁重联产生喷流结构，能量输运以阿尔芬模为主导。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 太阳针状体（spicule）等日冕加热与质量输运问题中，旋转型针状体事件的产生机制及其能量与物质输送通道尚不清晰，需要借助数值模拟厘清其驱动源、磁场拓扑演化及波模能量分配。

Method: 在初始背景强度10 G的向上（正极性）开放磁场中进行三维辐射磁流体力学（3D RMHD）模拟，识别涡旋关联的针状体类事件并选取典型事例详细分析；追踪光球涡旋—磁龙卷形成—磁重联的演化过程，开展垂直方向力分析（压力梯度力与洛伦兹力）以及波模分解，并统计4 Mm高度的质量通量与坡印廷通量。

Result: 事件发生前光球出现涡旋并扭转磁力线，向上发展形成磁龙卷系统；在磁龙卷外围，具有负垂直分量的扭转磁力线与外部正极性背景场及中心正极性场发生磁重联，重联区随磁龙卷上升并旋转，众多喷流子结构源于重联区等离子体向上抛射。力分析表明：光球与低色球等离子体抬升由压力梯度力主导，高色球则洛伦兹力逐渐占优。在日面以上约4 Mm处，平均向上质量通量密度约10⁻⁷ kg m⁻² s⁻¹，平均向上坡印廷通量密度约525 W m⁻²。波模分解显示能量输运以阿尔芬模为主，阿尔芬波、快模波与慢模波向低日冕输运能量比例约为10:7:3。

Conclusion: 旋转型针状体类事件由光球涡旋演化而成的磁龙卷及其外围磁重联驱动，其质量与能量向上输运显著，且以阿尔芬波为主导能量载体，为色球—日冕物质与能量供给提供了机制性解释。

Abstract: We performed three-dimensional radiative magnetohydrodynamic(3D RMHD) simulations in an upward-directed (positive-polarity) open magnetic field with an initial background strength of 10 G. We identified vortex-associated spicule-like events and carried out a detailed analysis of one representative event. Prior to the formation of this spicule-like event, a photospheric vortex appears and twists the magnetic field lines, then develops upward into higher atmospheric layers, forming a magnetic tornado system. This event is generated during this process. At the periphery of this magnetic tornado, twisted magnetic field lines with negative vertical components reconnect with both external positive-polarity background fields and central positive-polarity fields. The magnetic reconnection sites rise and rotate together with the developed magnetic tornado, and many jet-like substructures in this event are closely associated with plasma upward ejections from these reconnection regions. A vertical force analysis shows that, for this event, plasma uplift in the photosphere and lower chromosphere is dominated by the pressure-gradient force, whereas in the upper chromosphere, the Lorentz force gradually becomes dominant. At a height of about 4 Mm above the solar surface, this event produces a mean upward mass flux density on the order of $10^{-7}~{\rm kg~m^{-2}~s^{-1}}$ and a mean upward Poynting flux density of approximately $525~{\rm W~m^{-2}}$. The wave-mode decomposition results show that the energy transport associated with the rotational spicule-like event is dominated by the Alfvén mode. Alfvén waves, fast-mode waves, and slow-mode waves carry energy into the lower corona in an approximate ratio of $10:7:3$.

### [2] [X-Shooter view of planetary nebulae microstructures I: The case of NGC 7009 knots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13587)
*L. Konstantinou,S. Akras,K. Bouvis,P. Boumis,J. Garcia-Rojas,D. R. Gonçalves,K. N. Gourgouliatos*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 利用X-Shooter对NGC 7009的低电离结构(LISs)进行首次专用宽波段光谱研究，探测到250余条发射线及9条H2振转线，揭示其由激波主导。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LISs以低电离态物种发射增强为特征，但其起源仍不明确，且其分子气体含量几乎未被探索。

Method: 使用X-Shooter光谱仪获取NGC 7009 LISs从紫外到近红外的宽波段光谱，经ESO Reflex流程处理，并用最新版PyNeb推导物理性质。

Result: 探测到250余条发射线，包括Fe+、Ni+等低电离态禁线及首次在LISs中发现的P+两条跃迁；基于碰撞激发线与光学复合线得出电子温度和密度；近红外探测到9条H2振转发射线（7条首次报道），并构建H2激发图。

Conclusion: 多重激波示踪物及[Fe II]、[O I]的激波激发诊断支持LISs中存在激波；分子氢的激发最可能由激波碰撞过程主导。

Abstract: The properties of NGC 7009 knots are investigated by employing UV-to-near-infrared data from the X-Shooter spectrograph. These structures belong to the category of low-ionization structures (LISs), which are characterized by enhanced emission from low-ionization species and whose origin remains enigmatic. Our main goal is to conduct the first dedicated spectroscopic study of LISs using X-Shooter, that covers a wide spectral range from the UV to the near-IR, simultaneously. As a pilot study, we focus on NGC 7009 to investigate its ionized, atomic, and molecular gas, aiming to better characterize the physical properties of its LISs and explore their largely unexplored molecular content. The spectrum was processed through the ESO Reflex pipeline, and the physical properties of LISs were derived with the latest version of PyNeb. More than 250 emission lines were detected in the spectra of NGC 7009 LISs, including rare, forbidden transitions from low- ionization species such as Fe+ and Ni+, as well as two transitions from P+, identified here for the first time in LISs. The detection of multiple shock tracers, together with the shock excitation of [Fe II] and [O I], as revealed by emission-line diagnostics, supports the presence of shocks at LISs. The richness of the spectrum allowed us to derive electron temperatures and densities at the LISs using both collisionally excited and optical recombination lines. In the near-IR spectrum of the LISs, nine ro-vibrational H2 emission lines were detected, seven of which were reported here for the first time. These lines were used to construct the H2 excitation diagram, providing new insights into the physical conditions of the nebular gas. The excitation of molecular hydrogen is most likely dominated by collisional processes through shocks.

### [3] [Magnetic connectivity from the Sun to the Earth with MHD models II. Impact of input magnetic maps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13620)
*Barbara Perri,Silke Kennis,Brigitte Schmieder,Stefaan Poedts*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 本文量化了输入光球磁场图对磁连通性估计的影响：使用四家供应商的20幅磁图，对2019年7月（CR 2219）进行MHD建模并与ACE原位数据对比。结果显示输入磁图的选择显著影响连通性估计，全局一致率从28%到63%不等；HMI和GONG表现最佳，HCS位置是最关键参数。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 连通性估计需要快速计算，通常依赖半经验模型。前期工作表明优化后的MHD模型也能导出磁连通性且效果良好，尤其对复杂构型。但建模并非唯一影响因素——无论半经验还是MHD模型，其结果都高度依赖驱动模拟的输入数据。因此，需要定量评估不同输入磁图对连通性估计的影响。

Method: 选取2019年7月（第2219个卡林顿自转，活动极小期，干扰少但含高速流和多次极性反转）作为研究时段。使用来自四家供应商的20幅不同磁场图驱动MHD模型进行连通性计算。将结果与ACE卫星原位数据对比，并开发多种可视化技术，不仅在特定日期而且在完整卡林顿自转尺度上验证连通性。

Result: 输入磁图的选择对连通性估计影响巨大：全局一致率最低仅28%，最高达63%。HMI和GONG磁图给出一致的较优连通性估计，整个CR的正确率超过50%。对此时段，GONG-ADAPT磁图结果最差，得分低于35%（与此前发现COCONUT需针对该类磁图调整参数一致）。而WSO磁图虽日冕一致性较差，却给出良好的连通性估计，说明日球电流片（HCS）位置才是连通性最关键参数。此外，覆盖完整卡林顿自转的一致数据优于逐日数据。

Conclusion: 输入光球磁场图的选择显著影响磁连通性估计，全局一致率在28%–63%之间；HMI与GONG磁图表现最稳定，HCS位置是连通性的决定性参数。应优先采用覆盖完整卡林顿自转的一致性数据，而非逐日数据。

Abstract: Connectivity estimation needs to be fast and thus usually relies on semi-empirical models. We previously established that optimised magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) models can also be used to derive magnetic connectivity, yielding good results, especially for complex configurations. However, the modelling is not the only factor that can impact the connectivity estimation. Models' results, both semi-empirical and MHD, depend heavily on the input data used to drive the simulation. In order to quantify this impact on our connectivity estimation, we used 20 different maps by four providers. We selected the period of July 2019, which corresponds to Carrington rotation (CR) number 2219, because it had already been studied in a previous paper. It corresponds to a period of minimum activity, meaning minimal interference from transient structures, but with a high-speed stream and several polarity switches. We have compared our results with in situ data from ACE and developed several visualisation techniques to validate connectivity not only for a specific date but also for a full CR. The choice of the input magnetic map drastically affects the connectivity estimates, with global scores going from 28% global agreement to 63% in the most extreme case. The HMI and GONG maps consistently gave the best connectivity estimations, with more than 50% correct results of the full CR. For this specific period, the GONG-ADAPT maps yielded the least reliable results, with a score below 35%. This is not surprising, as we have shown before that COCONUT required parameter adjustments for these maps. However, WSO maps which showed poor coronal agreement actually provided good connectivity estimates, showing that the Heliospheric Current Sheet (HCS) location is in the end the most crucial parameter for connectivity. Consistent data over a full Carrington rotation are to be favored compared to daily.
