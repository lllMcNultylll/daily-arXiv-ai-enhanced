Method: 使用GREGOR望远镜的GRIS仪器观测了5个接近日面中心的太阳黑子，通过HAZEL2光谱偏振反演代码分析Si 10827 Å和He 10830 Å谱线，采用单色球层分量和双色球层分量两种反演策略，比较不同模型对斯托克斯谱线轮廓的拟合效果。

Result: 单分量反演显示：3个黑子在激波期间磁场显著增强，2个黑子磁场显著减弱。双分量反演（两个色球层平板模型）即使假设恒定磁场也能提供优异拟合，表明观测到的谱线轮廓变化可能源于强烈的速度梯度，其中He 10830 Å谱线同时对激波前沿两侧敏感。

Conclusion: 观测到的激波期间斯托克斯谱线轮廓变化存在两种竞争性解释：真实的磁场强度波动或强烈的速度梯度效应。两种模型都能解释观测数据，需要进一步研究来确定哪种机制占主导地位。

Abstract: Umbral flashes are manifestations of magnetoacoustic shocks in the solar chromosphere. These phenomena are thought to influence the evolution of chromospheric umbral magnetic fields. However, the impact of these shocks on inferred chromospheric magnetic field oscillations remains unclear. We examined five different sunspots located near the solar disk center, observed with the GRIS instrument installed at the GREGOR telescope. The HAZEL2 Spectropolarimetric inversion code is used to obtain the photospheric and chromospheric line-of-sight velocities and magnetic fields in Si 10827 Å and He 10830 Å spectral lines, respectively, using various inversion strategies. In the inversions with one chromospheric component, three of the sunspots exhibit remarkably stronger magnetic fields accompanying the shocks, while the other two sunspots show striking reductions in the magnetic field. Alternatively, the Stokes profiles can be reproduced by models with two chromospheric slabs, one on top of the other, through two-component inversions. These inversions provide excellent fits even when magnetic field fluctuations are discarded by imposing a constant magnetic field during the whole temporal series. In this scenario, the observed Stokes profiles are interpreted as the result of strong velocity gradients, where the He 10830 Å line is sensitive to both sides of the shock front. Both competing models explaining the spectral profiles during the shocks, either large magnetic field fluctuations or velocity gradients, are critically discussed.
