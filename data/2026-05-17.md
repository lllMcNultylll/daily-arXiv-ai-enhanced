### [1] [Analysis of DQZ White Dwarf Evolution through Procyon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14012)
*Momin Y. Khan,Barbara G. Castanheira*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 利用MESA恒星演化模型网格，结合精确动力学质量与光谱观测，系统约束了南河三星系（Procyon A & B）的演化参数，得到了最佳拟合模型（包括质量、年龄、白矮星冷却年龄），并建立了初始-终末质量关系，发现核心超射高于标准范围。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Procyon是研究非相互作用双星恒星演化的理想系统，但对其系统年龄、B星前身星质量以及初始-终末质量关系的精确约束仍存在不足，需要系统量化金属丰度、混合长度和核心超射等参数的影响。

Method: 构建了大范围MESA恒星演化轨迹网格，以精确动力学质量和光谱有效温度及光度为约束，系统变化初始参数（金属丰度Z、混合长度α、核心超射β），寻找H-R图上的最佳拟合模型，并考虑白矮星表面重金属吸积过程拟合光谱同位素丰度。

Result: 最佳拟合得到A星质量1.487±0.095 M☉，B星质量0.592±0.082 M☉，系统年龄2.23±0.90 Gyr，白矮星冷却年龄1.20±0.49 Gyr；发现核心超射β=0.5-1.0高于标准范围；将Procyon B映射到1.9-2.6 M☉前身星范围的初始-终末质量关系。

Conclusion: Procyon系统的最佳演化模型与独立测定结果一致，核心超射需采用高于标准范围的值，并建立了氢缺陷白矮星的初始-终末质量关系。

Abstract: Procyon is a great system to probe stellar evolution of non-interacting binaries. We present an extensive grid of MESA (Modules for Experiments in Stellar Astrophysics) evolutionary tracks to constrain the evolution of Procyon A and B. We systematically vary the initial parameters of our grid anchored by precise dynamical masses and spectroscopic determinations of effective temperature (T$_{eff}$) and luminosity ($L$) to match the stars' positions on the H-R Diagram. Our goal is two-fold: (i) to quantify how the inferred system age and the progenitor mass of Procyon B depend on metallicity ($Z$), mixing length ($α$), and core overshoot ($β$), and (ii) to determine the best fitting model of Procyon B within a model-based initial-to-final mass relationship (IMFR) for hydrogen-deficient white dwarfs. Our best-fit models reproduce the observed properties for both components, yielding $M_\mathrm{A}=1.487 \pm 0.095$ M$_{\odot}$, $M_\mathrm{B}=0.592\pm 0.082$ M$_{\odot}$, a system age of $2.23 \pm 0.90$ Gyr, and a white dwarf cooling age of $1.20\pm0.49$ Gyr for Procyon B, consistent with independent determinations. Our results point to higher core overshoot than the standard adopted range, with the best fits ranging from $β=0.5-1.0$. From our model grid, we map Procyon B to the initial-to-final mass relationship for H-deficient white dwarfs in the $1.9\!-\!2.6$ M$_{\odot}$ progenitor range. Additionally, we implement the accretion of heavy metals onto the surface of the WD and fit our isotopic abundances to spectroscopic observations. We outline the physics used in our analysis.

### [2] [Spectral Disentangling Reveals Deep CNO-cycle Exposure in ET Cru](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14018)
*Gökhan Yücel,Volkan Bakış,Christian Nitschelm,Timur Şahin,Ferhat Güney*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 本文通过综合光谱和光度分析，精确测定了半分离双星系统ET Cru的物理参数和化学丰度，发现次星存在极端碳耗竭和氮富集，为深层次CNO循环暴露提供了直接光谱证据，并将该系统确立为研究大质量恒星演化和双星相互作用的基准Algol型双星。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 利用经历物质转移的双星系统作为检验恒星演化的独特实验室，通过高精度测量揭示双星相互作用对恒星内部结构和化学演化的影响。

Method: 采用光谱解混技术独立测定双星两颗子星的有效温度和化学丰度，结合多波段光谱能量分布（SED）建模进行距离测定，并与Gaia DR3视差进行比较。

Result: 主星质量13.41 M☉，次星质量6.00 M☉（不确定度~1.3%）；半径分别为5.58 R☉和5.68 R☉（精度0.4-0.5%）；表面重力精度优于1%，有效温度精度3-5%。次星表现出极端化学异常，碳耗竭和氮富集程度远超经典Algol系统。SED模型距离~2.5 kpc，与Gaia DR3视差存在不一致。

Conclusion: ET Cru被确立为基准Algol型双星系统，提供了深层次CNO循环暴露的直接光谱证据，确认主星为返老还童的质量增益星，为理解大质量恒星晚期演化和先进双星相互作用提供了化学和动力学上的典型案例。

Abstract: Binary stars undergoing mass transfer provide unique laboratories for testing stellar evolution. Here, we present a comprehensive photometric and spectroscopic analysis of the semi-detached system ET Cru. Using spectral disentangling, we independently determined the effective temperatures and chemical abundances of both components with high precision, including nine elements (eleven species). We find masses of $13.41\,M_\odot$ and $6.00\,M_\odot$ for the primary and secondary, respectively, with uncertainties of only $\sim$1.3%. The radii are $5.58\,R_\odot$ and $5.68\,R_\odot$, measured to within 0.4% and 0.5%. Surface gravities are constrained to better than 1%, while effective temperatures are determined to within 3-5%. The secondary exhibits extreme chemical anomalies, with severe carbon depletion and nitrogen enrichment far exceeding those reported in classical Algol systems. Multi-wavelength spectral energy distribution modelling yields a distance of $\sim$2.5 kpc, inconsistent with the $Gaia$ DR3 parallax, suggesting systematic astrometric uncertainties in the parallax distance. Together, these results establish ET Cru as a benchmark Algol-type binary, revealing direct spectroscopic evidence of deep CNO-cycle exposure in the donor and confirming the primary star as a rejuvenated gainer. ET Cru thus provides a chemically and dynamically illustrative case for understanding advanced binary interactions and the late evolutionary stages of massive-star evolution.

### [3] [SN2023ixf: ultraviolet-to-infrared radiative-transfer modeling of the nebular-phase evolution until 1000 days](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14081)
*Luc Dessart,Wynn V. Jacobson-Galan,K. Azalee Bostroem,Alexei V. Filippenko,WeiKang Zheng,Thomas G. Brink,Lluis Galbany,Claudia Gutierrez,Stefano Valenti*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 本文对SN2023ixf超新星在星云阶段至1000天的非局部热动平衡辐射转移进行了建模，发现其与星周物质的相互作用持续存在，尘埃形成（碳和硅混合）在冷密壳层和内抛射物中逐渐增加，最终质量可达1e-4太阳质量，紫外辐射受铁线及莱曼α和MgII2800的蓝移发射影响，冷密壳层持续减速，质量增加约0.1太阳质量。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 理解SN2023ixf在星云阶段的长期演化，特别是与星周物质相互作用、尘埃形成过程及其对光变曲线和光谱的影响。

Method: 采用非局部热动平衡辐射转移建模，使用与光球层演化匹配的抛射物模型（初始15太阳质量红超巨星，抛射物质量7-8太阳质量，动能1.2e51尔格，56镍质量0.05太阳质量），附加质量0.2太阳质量、速度8000公里/秒的冷密壳层。

Result: 星周相互作用在所有时期持续存在，紫外波段始终受其影响，但光学波段在约600天后才被主导；V波段光变曲线需增强伽马射线逃逸和尘埃形成；尘埃质量在冷密壳层和内抛射物中持续上升，可能达到1e-4太阳质量（700天）；冷密壳层从112天的8000公里/秒减速至998天的约6500公里/秒；紫外辐射受铁线以及莱曼α和MgII2800的蓝移发射影响。

Conclusion: SN2023ixf与SN1993J在1-3年阶段有许多相似之处，但由于尘埃消光和更低的温度（弱[NII]和无[OIII]线），其最终更暗，这可能是更大冷密壳层和抛射物质量的结果。

Abstract: We present non-local thermodynamic equilibrium radiative-transfer modeling of SN2023ixf during the nebular phase out to 1000d, using the same ejecta that matched its photospheric evolution, namely a partially stripped red-supergiant star of initially 15Msun whose terminal explosion yielded ejecta with 7-8Msun, kinetic energy of 1.2e51erg, and 56Ni mass of 0.05Msun, augmented with a cold dense shell (CDS) of 0.2Msun at 8000km/s. Interaction with circumstellar material persists at all epochs, powering the ultraviolet (UV) flux at all times, but dominating the optical only after ~600d. Matching the V-band light curve requires invoking both enhanced gamma-ray escape and dust formation after ~200d, first in the CDS and eventually in the inner ejecta as well. Depending on where they form relative to the dust, emission lines are uniformly attenuated or skewed with a blue-red asymmetry. Our models suggest a rising dust mass (chosen as an C-rich and Si-rich mixture) in the CDS and inner ejecta, possibly reaching 1e-4Msun at 700d, while an external cold dust component is required to match the mid-infrared emission. The UV radiation, largely unaffected by dust, is influenced by the emission and absorption from Fe lines, together with strong, blueshifted emission from Lyalpha and MgII2800, both present at >~200d and with a strengthening fractional flux thereafter. Optical-depth effects play a critical role for the UV flux, and most notably on Lyalpha whose strength depends strongly on the CDS structure (mass and extent) and the treatment of power injection. The CDS is continuously slowing down from 8000km/s at 112d to ~6500km/s at 998d, suggesting a growth in mass of several 0.1Msun. SN2023ixf shares many similarities with SN1993J at 1-3yr, but it is eventually fainter due to dust extinction and cooler (i.e., weak [NII] and no [OIII] lines) likely as a result of greater CDS and ejecta masses.

### [4] [Direct Evidence of Non-Ideal Dissipative Dynamics in Solar Wind Magnetic Switchbacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14114)
*Forrest Mozer,Oleksiy Agapitov,Kyung-Eun Choi,Richard Sydora*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 本文通过帕克太阳探测器的电场测量数据（13-40太阳半径），证明太阳风中的磁开关（switchbacks）是Hall-MHD结构而非理想MHD结构，并发现其内部存在增强的三分量Poynting矢量，表明开关是活跃的非MHD过程，而非简单的向外传播脉冲，这解决了关于开关物理本质的争议，并为其起源争议（太阳表面vs太阳风原位演化）提供了新证据。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 解决关于太阳风磁开关的两个根本性争议：(1) 其物理描述应使用理想MHD还是Hall-MHD；(2) 其起源是太阳表面还是太阳风中原位产生。

Method: 利用帕克太阳探测器在13-40太阳半径范围内测量的电场数据，分析开关结构在等离子体参考系中的电场特征及其伴随的Poynting矢量分量。

Result: 开关结构在等离子体参考系中存在非零电场，证明其为Hall-MHD结构；开关内部存在增强的三分量Poynting矢量，且各分量量级相当，这与简单向外传播脉冲的模型相矛盾。

Conclusion: 年轻太阳风中的磁开关是非MHD过程，具有活跃的原位演化特征，为天体等离子体中的能量输运和耗散研究提供了新框架。

Abstract: Magnetic switchbacks, large-amplitude, localized Alfvenic like rotations of the solar wind magnetic field, have been the subject of intensive investigation, with approximately 200 refereed papers published in the last decade. Yet, fundamental controversies persist regarding whether switchbacks can be described with Ideal MHD (magnetohydrodynamic) physics or Hall-MHD physics and whether their origin is at the solar surface or in the solar wind. To settle these controversies, we present Parker Solar Probe electric field measurements between 13 and 40 solar radii, which show that switchbacks have non-zero electric fields in the plasma frame, a finding that definitively settles the physics controversy by proving that switchbacks are Hall-MHD, not Ideal MHD, structures. Along with these electric fields, there are enhanced Poynting vectors having three components with similar magnitudes that exist only inside the switchbacks. These facts contradict the view of switchbacks as simple outward-propagating pulses. Together, they resolve one controversy by showing that switchbacks in the young solar wind are a non-MHD process. They contribute to the second (source) controversy by identifying switchbacks as sites of active, in-situ, evolution. These findings provide a new framework for understanding energy transport and dissipation in astrophysical plasmas.

</details>
