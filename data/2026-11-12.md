Motivation: B型星的共转磁层受磁场强度与自转速度强烈影响，决定了可见光与射电辐射的几何与强度；但热光度低于log(L/L⊙)~2.8时Hα发射基本消失，因此ρ Oph C这类最低光度的快速自转磁B型星是理解磁层下限与辐射机制的关键，而其磁场与Hα磁层此前仅有快照观测约束。

Method: 获取覆盖完整自转周期的高分辨率ESPaDOnS分光偏振数据；结合Hipparcos、K2与TESS测光重新确定自转周期；用Stokes参数建模磁场强度与几何；比较Hα等值宽变化、金属线等值宽与磁层模型同光变曲线的关系，并与离心突破（CBO）模型预测对比射电、光度蚀深与Hα强度。

Result: 磁场强度与几何与射电数据推断模型相符；Hα几乎无变，符合磁场模型预期；光变主要来自表面化学丰度斑，但存在约mmag量级、由磁层起源的复杂自转调制变化；其磁层Hα发射是目前探测到的最弱、光度最低的B型星案例，明显弱于CBO模型预测，测光蚀深也远低于CBO预测，而射电光度与CBO预测一致。

Conclusion: ρ Oph C是迄今探测到磁层Hα发射的最低光度B型星，其弱Hα与浅蚀深偏离CBO模型而射电一致，提示它可能是氢主导风与解耦金属风两个有效温度区间之间的过渡天体。

Abstract: The corotating magnetospheres of B-type stars are strongly affected by magnetic field strength and rotational velocity, which determine both the geometry and strength of visible and radio emission; however, H$α$ emission largely disappears below a bolometric luminosity of $\log{(L / L_\odot)} \sim 2.8$. The nearby B5 V star $ρ$ Oph C (HD 147932) is the least-luminous rapidly rotating magnetic B-type star to display magnetospheric H$α$ emission. While $ρ$ Oph C's radio magnetosphere has been extensively studied, its magnetic field and H$α$ magnetosphere have been constrained only via snapshot observations. We present a new dataset of high-resolution ESPaDOnS spectropolarimetry covering the star's rotational period, which is revisited by combining Hipparcos, K2 and TESS photometry. We find that the star's magnetic field strength and geometry are consistent with the model inferred from radio data. As expected from the magnetic model, there is very little variability in H$α$. Comparison of the light curve to both equivalent width measurements from metallic lines and magnetospheric models suggests that the star's photometric variability is dominated by surface chemical abundance spots; however, there is complex rotationally modulated variability at about the mmag level which probably originates in the magnetosphere. $ρ$ Oph C is the least luminous B-type star in which magnetospheric H$α$ emission has yet been detected, and its emission is notably weaker than the predictions of the centrifugal breakout model. Its photometric eclipse depth is also much lower than predicted by the CBO model. However, its radio luminosity is consistent with CBO predictions. We speculate on this basis that $ρ$ Oph C may be a transitional object between effective temperature regimes with hydrogen-dominated and decoupled metallic winds.
